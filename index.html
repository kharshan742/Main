Rithanya…

like the first rains of June —
unexpected, gentle,

you were right beside me. Distance tried its best —
it gave us longing,
it gave us waiting,
it gave us the ache
of a hug we can't have yet.

separated us. I want you to know this,

I am not going anywhere.
This love is not a season.

It deepens. I will come to you.
I will hold your hand
in every room, in every city,

I choose you.

and on the days we have nothing

I ever want to walk into.
You are my forever. One day soon,
there will be no more goodbyes,
no more counting the days,
no more phone screens between us —
just you and me,
in the same home,
building the same beautiful life. I promise you a future.
I promise you forever.
I promise to love you
the way you deserve to be loved —
completely and without end.
